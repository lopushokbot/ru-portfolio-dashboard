Notes

    Methodology & sources

    Prices are the official closing prices of the Moscow Exchange main session (MOEX ISS), refreshed every morning at 8:00 Dubai. Trailing EPS is IFRS earnings per share over the last twelve months — the last four quarters, or the last two half-years for semi-annual reporters (LUKOIL, NOVATEK, Tatneft, Norilsk Nickel) — from smart-lab.ru, cross-checked against T-Investments (T-Bank) fundamentals; disagreements above 25% are flagged in the Notes. Forward EPS is dohod.ru's forecast of net income over the next twelve months, divided by the same share count as trailing EPS so both multiples are like-for-like.

    Index multiples are rebuilt from scratch on every run: P/E = 1 ÷ Σ weight × (EPS ÷ price) over all MOEX Russia Index constituents at their published free-float weights — equivalent to Σ float-adjusted market cap ÷ Σ float-adjusted earnings. Loss-makers are included, as index providers do; the tile also shows the figure without them. Ordinary and preferred shares (SBER/SBERP, TATN/TATNP, SNGS/SNGSP) each carry their own weight and price against the same company EPS.

    Dividend yield = dividends with a record date in the last twelve months ÷ today's price (T-Investments dividend register); next dividend = the next declared or board-recommended payment. Target = T-Investments analyst consensus 12-month target price. Yield gap = the index earnings yield (1 ÷ P/E) minus the 10-year OFZ zero-coupon yield (MOEX G-curve). History bands = smart-lab quarterly trailing P/E over five years. No Yahoo Finance data is used — its Russian quotes and share counts have been unreliable since 2022. Numbers are for reference only — not investment advice.
